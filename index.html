How I think about this work

Therapy for me is more than managing symptoms.

There are parts of you that have not had the safety to feel fully seen yet. That's usually where we begin. I don't bring a specific formula to this work. We are not meant to fit ourselves into frameworks to experience healing. Frameworks are meant to be adaptable to our stories, our experiences, our values and, that's exactly what I aim to facilitate through my work.

About Ridhima

"I reflect, question, and sometimes push you. But it's always your space, your story. I work alongside you, never ahead of you."

I'm Ridhima, a counselling psychologist practicing in Mumbai & online. My practice is integrative: I draw most often from Narrative Therapy and Internal Family Systems, with grounding in trauma-informed and humanistic care.

What that means in plainer language: I don't approach you with a protocol. I listen for the parts of you that have been managing the most, and for the stories that got built around them. And I help you build a healthier relationship with both. I work with curiosity, not certainty.

My practice is queer-affirmative, sensitive to caste, faith, and culture, and held in the belief that healing happens at the pace of your nervous system.
